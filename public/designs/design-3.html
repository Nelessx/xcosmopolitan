          Our story

          Beauty,unhurried

          Xcosmopolitan is a unisex salon in Nagpokhari, Naxal, for skin, hair and nails. We work strictly by appointment, so the studio stays calm and nobody is rushed.

          Why we workby appointment

          Walk-in salons often mean waiting around, and a stylist with one eye on the door. Working by appointment lets us plan each visit properly: who you'll see, how long it takes and what you'd like done.

          Our team covers skin, hair, colour, men's grooming, nails and makeup, so you can get more done in one visit. And if a treatment won't help, we'd rather tell you than sell it to you.
